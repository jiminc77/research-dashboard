## Late-acceleration findings

- **V1 s0 is a late improver.** It is only +0.070 at 150.5k, then reaches +1.426 at 225.3k and +1.722 final. A 150k kill at return <0.1 would falsely reject it.
- **V1 s1 is even later and nonmonotonic.** It remains negative through 275.5k and jumps to +1.032 at final. Any 150k kill at return <0 would reject a run that ultimately becomes positive.
- **V1 s2 is not good from initialization, but enters the good basin earlier.** Return is near zero through 100k, +0.805 at 150.5k, +1.343 at 175.1k, and +1.960 at 225.3k. It is an earlier accelerator than s0/s1, not an initially dominant seed.
- First crossing sensitivity for V1: return >0 occurs at ~100k/300k/100k for s0/s1/s2; return >1 occurs at ~225k/300k/175k. “Late acceleration” must therefore be defined with both threshold and sustained-window criteria.
- Selection manifests choose 250.9k/225.3k/300.0k for V1 s0/s1/s2, while the original in-training evaluations at the first two selected transitions are noisy and worse than their final rows. Re-evaluation-based checkpoint choice adds another lottery opportunity.
- Success and continuous return do not have a fixed lead/lag: s0 return is positive by 100k but success remains 8%; s1 obtains 5–6% success while return is still negative and only becomes positive at final; s2 rises in both after 150k.

## Early-stop rule assessment

- 25–100k: technical criteria only. All three V1 runs are near-zero/negative and cannot be separated safely.
- 150k: no hard kill. Threshold `<0.1` falsely rejects s0 and s1 (2/3); threshold `<0` still falsely rejects s1 (1/3). Leave-one-seed-out fitting with only three V1 seeds is too underpowered to estimate a stable harm margin.
- 225k: the brief’s conjunctive rule (both seeds worse in return AUC and final-D AUC, nonpositive slope, plus diagnostic collapse) is directionally safer, but the numeric harm margin remains OPEN; V1 s1 demonstrates that even a negative 175–225k slope can recover at 300k.
- Use late-window AUC/mean, paired BB deltas, worst-seed guard, and all-attempt disclosure. Never use a best single checkpoint as winner metric.

## V2 design implication

The observed failure is basin-entry timing/lower-tail behavior, not a simple low ceiling. Candidate screening must preserve s2’s earlier good basin while rescuing s0/s1, and cannot use 150k hard futility. Objective/contact-margin diagnostics should be aligned to the pre/post acceleration windows identified here.
